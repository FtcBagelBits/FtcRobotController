# Drive coach: match strategy

The drive coach keeps time, watches **both** hives, and calls the plan out loud. Every few seconds, ask:
**Which action most improves our chance of winning from here: shooting, helping our partner,
defending, or parking?**

## The match clock (AUTO 0:30, 8 s break, TELEOP 2:00)

| When | Coach does |
| --- | --- |
| Before | Partner talk (back page). Pick our auto: Primary or Secondary. |
| AUTO | Watch: did we tip? Did both robots LEAVE and PARK? Add up the SWARM points. |
| 8 s break | Driver: INIT, then START (PollenBot is already selected). Coach: first job, and **"SWARM done"** or **"SWARM needs 1 / 2 parks"**. |
| 2:00 - 1:00 | Collect and shoot. Keep counting both UP cells (below). |
| 1:00 | All remaining NECTAR can be entered now (Section 10.1). |
| 0:30 | **Decide the endgame** (table below) and call it. |
| park time + 3 s | Last call: **"PARK NOW"**, unless the plan is to stay. Our TeleOp drive to the loading zone: ______ s (time it at practice). |

## How close is each hive to tipping? Count out loud.

- Count pieces that land in **each** alliance's UP cell since its last tip: ours and theirs.
- Field-check numbers: the starting UP cell holds 3 staged nectar, and **about 3 more pollen tip it**.
  An empty cell holds 7 pollen; **the 8th tips it**. Nectar is heavier, so fewer pieces.
  These are setup checks, not promises: some launched pieces bounce out.
- After a tip the count starts over: the pieces fall out and the other, empty cell is UP.
- Call it like: **"Ours: 5 in, about 3 to tip. Theirs: 7 in, 1 to tip."**

## The endgame call (last 30 seconds)

| Situation | Call |
| --- | --- |
| We can finish a tip; they can't | **SHOOT.** Park only if the tip lands with park time left. |
| They are one shot from a tip; we aren't close | **DEFEND** that last shot. Denying 20 beats our 5 for parking, unless SWARM needs our park or we are safely ahead. |
| Both close | Do whichever is **more likely to work**. Our tip and their missed tip are both worth 20. |
| Neither close | **PARK** early and cleanly: 5 sure points. Shoot what's left in the robot first if there's time: each piece left in our UP cell is 2. |
| Ahead by more than they can still score | **PLAY SAFE.** Park. No fouls. |
| Behind by more than 20 | Only tips help. **Shoot to the end.** |

<div class="page-break"></div>

**Match 4 lesson:** Team 1 stayed on the field until the last seconds to make the shot that tipped and
won, knowing they'd have only seconds to park. When the other alliance needs one last shot and we
are not about to tip, our job is to **stay and block that shot, even if we lose our park points.**

## SWARM: know what we need, and say it

SWARM = LEAVE + PARK points of both robots together, **16 or more**. **End-of-match parks count**
(5 per robot), on top of AUTO's LEAVE 3 + AUTO PARK 5 per robot.
After AUTO, add it up and call what we still need:

| SWARM points after AUTO | What we need at the end |
| --- | --- |
| 16 or more | Nothing. SWARM is made. Our end park is just 5 points, so defending can be worth more. |
| 11, 13 or 15 | **One** robot parks at the end. |
| 6, 8 or 10 | **Both** robots park at the end. |
| 5 or less | Not reachable. Play for points. |

When SWARM depends on our park, parking is worth 5 points **plus 1 RP**. Park, unless staying is
what wins the match (a win is 3 RP).

## Defense: legal only (Game Manual section 11)

- **Pin for 3 s at most** (G421). Then back off at least 2 ft (61 cm) for 3 s. MAJOR FOUL per pin.
- **Never launch at their hive** to stop a tip (G417 lists this as a strategic violation).
- **No contact on their side during AUTO** (G402).
- Don't damage, tip over or tangle with them (G419, G420). Don't hold their NECTAR (G408) or keep
  them from more than 8 scoring pieces (G411). Don't shake the walls or flowers (G425).
- Good defense: get between their robot and the spot it shoots from, or between it and its pollen.
  Keep moving.
- At the drivers' meeting, ask if any area is off-limits for contact in TeleOp.

## Partner talk, before every match

1. **Autos:** who shoots first? We run **Primary** (we shoot first) or **Secondary** (they do).
   If they have no reliable auto shot, we are **Primary**.
2. **Lanes and parking:** Primary parks INNER, Secondary parks OUTER. Show the partner sheet.
3. **Their shooting:** how many balls per volley, how often they tip, how fast.
4. **TeleOp jobs:** who shoots, who collects or drains flowers, who defends.
5. **Endgame:** who parks and when; what we need for SWARM.

## Calls to use

**"TIME 30 / 20 / 10"** - **"OURS: _ TO TIP"** - **"THEIRS: _ TO TIP"** - **"SHOOT"** - **"DEFEND"** -
**"HELP"** (partner) - **"PARK NOW"** - **"SWARM DONE"** / **"SWARM NEEDS 1 / 2"**

Robot reminders for the driver: **R1** hold = shoot. **Circle** = auto-aim at our hive cell (relatively untested; right stick cancels). **Square** = drainer up, then **L1** hold = unjam. **Cross** = drainer down/up.
